\part{Calibration Aware Diffusion Residual Learning}

\section{Problem Formulation}

\subsection{Forecasting System}

Given:

$\hat y_t = f(y_{1:t-1})$

compute residuals:
$
r_t
=
y_t-\hat y_t
$
and learn:
$
p(r)
$
using diffusion models.

\subsection{Standard Residual Learning}

Current diffusion models optimize:
$
\mathcal L_{\text{diffusion}}
$
through score matching or denoising.

Calibration is evaluated only after training.


\subsection{Proposed Framework}

Calibration becomes part of the optimization objective:
$
\mathcal L
=
\mathcal L_{\text{diffusion}}
+
\lambda
\mathcal L_{\text{calibration}}
$

\section{Calibration Objectives}

\subsection{Coverage Loss}

Penalize deviations from nominal coverage:

$\mathcal L_{\text{coverage}}
=
(\hat C-(1-\alpha))^2
$

\subsection{PIT-Based Calibration Loss}

Encourage:
$
u_i
=
F_i(y_i)
$
to approach Uniform(0,1).


\subsection{ECE-Based Loss}

Penalize confidence-frequency mismatch.

\subsection{CRPS-Based Loss}

Use proper scoring rules as calibration objectives.



\subsection{Combined Objective}

Investigate:
$
\mathcal L
=
\mathcal L_{\text{diffusion}}
+
\lambda
\mathcal L_{\text{calibration}}
$
for different calibration choices.


\section{CADRL Training Procedure}

Step 1: Fit forecasting model.

Step 2: Extract residuals.

Step 3: Train diffusion residual model.

Step 4: Generate predictive residual samples.

Step 5: Compute predictive distributions.

Step 6: Evaluate calibration.

Step 7: Backpropagate calibration loss.

Step 8: Update diffusion model.
